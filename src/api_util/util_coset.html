Coset of a model#

Coset of a model: the SAMB of a model of H moved by a representative g of a coset g H in a supergroup P.

The data of the cosets are those of the group binaries (MultiPieGen): “coset” of the group file (the first coset in the direct parent) and “parent”, “coset” of info (the parents and the cosets in them); any other supergroup is reached through the direct supergroups (“supergroup” of info, util_supergroup).

  • Reduction of g (in the coordinates of H).

    • “rotation” (det g > 0): the image system is written in the frame moved by g; its data are those of the model, and g Z_j = +Z’_j (the SAMB of the image system with the same label).

    • “improper” (det g < 0, H not chiral): g = g’ m^-1 with an improper operation m of H and the rotation g’ = g m: g Z_j = sum_k D(m^-1)_kj Z’_k in the frame moved by g’ (D = 1 for the identity irrep).

    • “inversion” (det g < 0, H chiral): g = r I with the inversion I of the first coset in the direct parent and a rotation r: I maps H to H’ = I H I^-1 (map M), the sites and bonds of each Wyckoff position W to those of W’ (row i -> row perm[i], bond reversed if sign = -1), and the cluster SAMB by eps (label by label) or by U (within an irrep). The atomic SAMB of the orbitals of ranks (l1, l2) are multiplied by (-1)^(l1+l2). The frame is moved by r.

  • Image system of the inversion. Its representative (row 0) of each Wyckoff position is the image of row i0 with perm[i0] = 0 (image_representative); a model of H’ created from its own input may have other representative bonds, and the inversion model (model input “create_inversion_model”, inversion_model) has these representatives.

  • Parent structure (resolve_coset). The limit of the Wyckoff parameters at which the structure is invariant under P: “parent_limit” of the binaries for the direct parent, util_supergroup.parent_limit otherwise; for a supergroup that is not a parent of the binaries, the embedding whose parent structure is the nearest to the model. On it, g is a symmetry and acts on the matrix elements of the model (image_space, the atoms matched by their parent positions).

  • Weight. The weight of the eigenvalue w^m (w = exp(2 pi i/n), n the order of g modulo H) of Z_j is w_m(j) = (1/n) sum_t w^(-m t) <Z_j, g^t Z_j>/<Z_j, Z_j> (weight_from_space).

  • S and F. S^(t)_kj = <Z_k, g^t Z_j> (overlap_matrix) and n Pi^(m) = sum_t w^(-m t) S^(t) (projected_matrix) give <g^t Z_j> and n <Pi_m Z_j> for a state invariant under H if every g^t Z_j lies on the bonds of the model (“complete”, parent_shells); F_j = sum_{m>0} n <Pi_m Z_j> (MaterialModel.parent_forbidden_part).

image_representative(inv, site_dict, bond_dict, lattice=True)[source]#

Representative sites and bonds of the image system under the inversion (first coset of a chiral group).

The eps/U of the cluster SAMB (MultiPieGen) map row i of each Wyckoff position W to row perm[i] of W’ (bond reversed if sign = -1). The representative (row 0) of the image is therefore M (row i0) with perm[i0] = 0, which is M (representative) when perm[0] = 0.

parse_coset(group, spec)[source]#

Representatives of the cosets to analyze.

Parameters:

  • group (Group) – group H of the model.

  • spec (None, False, dict or list) – “coset” of the model input.

Returns:

  • (list) – [(parent id, representative tag or None, embedding or None)], [] for False, or for None and a group without a direct parent.

Note

  • None: the representative of the first coset in the direct parent.

  • {“parent”: id, “rep”: tag}: for a parent of the binaries (info[“parent”][H]), the operation must be one of the cosets of info[“coset”][H][parent] (not a translation only, nor an operation of H; default: the first).

  • any supergroup P (Group.supergroups), or with “embedding” (index of Group.embedding): “rep” is an element of P in the coordinates of H (default: the first coset other than H modulo the lattice of H, the inversion if any); the embedding is the one whose parent structure is the nearest to the model if not given.

projected_matrix(sp_, n)[source]#

Matrices of the projections on the eigenvalues w^m of g (order n, w = exp(2 pi i/n)) in the SAMB of the model.

Returns:

  • (list) – [n Pi^(m)_kj = sum_t w^(-m t) S^(t)_kj for m = 0..n-1], so that n <Pi_m Z_j> = sum_k (n Pi^(m))_kj <Z_k> for a state invariant under H.

  • (bool) – complete (for every power of g).

resolve_coset(mm, parent=None, rep=None, embedding=None, require_structure=True)[source]#

The parent, the embedding, the representative g, and the parent structure used by image_space.

Parameters:

  • mm (MaterialModel) – model (analyzed).

  • parent (str, optional) – supergroup P (default: the direct parent of the binaries).

  • rep (str or array, optional) – element g of P (“point operation of P:[translation in H]” or 4x4 in the coordinates of H; default: the first coset of the binaries for its parents, the first coset other than H modulo the lattice of H (the inversion if any, a translation only if no other) otherwise).

  • embedding (None, int, dict or array, optional) – embedding of H in P (util_supergroup.resolve_embedding); default: the parents of the binaries with their transformation, otherwise the embedding whose parent structure is the nearest to the model.

  • require_structure (bool, optional) – raise an error if no embedding gives a parent structure of the model (otherwise the first embedding, with “parent_structure” and “lim_pos” None and the reason in “note”).

Returns:

  • (dict) – “parent”, “rep”, “g”, “order”, “frame” (_Frame), “limits”, “embedding” (None for the binaries), “parent_structure”, “lim_pos”, “md”.

samb_map(mm, coef)[source]#

{z_j: [(z_k, A_jk)]} from the image vectors in the label space.

Parameters:

  • mm (MaterialModel) – model.

  • coef (dict) – {z: {(UniqueSAMBType, t1, c1, t2, c2): coefficient}}, image of Z_j in the labels of the image system (the same UniqueSAMBType as the model for the SAMB of the image system with the same label).

Note

  • g Z_j = sum_k A_kj Z’_k is solved in the label space by least squares: the label vectors of the SAMB are not normalized in general (e.g., the norm is 1/2 for a hybrid of the orbitals of different ranks, whose hermitian partner has no label of its own).
